Design a modern, clean, premium multi-page website for Rubi Pictures — a Kenyan film and media production company. The site should feel world-class, editorial, and minimal — inspired by major global media outlets. Every page shares the same top utility bar and navigation.

GLOBAL STYLE

Background: pure white #FFFFFF throughout every page
Primary text: bold black #000000
Supporting text: regular black or dark gray #1a1a1a
Accent / CTA gradient: deep wine/burgundy #8B0000 blending into warm coral-orange #FF6B35 — left to right, applied to all buttons
Font: clean modern sans-serif — Inter or Helvetica Neue
No dark backgrounds anywhere on any page
Generous whitespace — let every element breathe
GLOBAL HEADER — appears on every single page, always sticky

Layer 1 — Top Utility Bar

Slim horizontal bar, white background, subtle bottom border #e0e0e0
Lists partner names as clickable text links, evenly spaced with | separators
Partners: Al Jazeera | IFRC | CGTN | DW | Corps Africa | AfriStar | SGN
Clicking any partner name navigates to the Work page with that partner's projects pre-filtered
Font size: 12–13px, clean, light weight
Always visible on scroll across all pages
Layer 2 — Main Navigation Bar

White background, sits directly below the utility bar, also sticky
Left: Rubi Pictures logo placeholder (clean bold text if no file)
Right nav links: Home About Work Services Contact
Far right: "Contact Us" CTA button — wine-to-coral gradient, white bold text, rounded corners
Thin bottom shadow separates nav from page content
